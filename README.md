Project Brief :

"
1. Project Name:
Semi Colon – Digital Gift Card Marketplace

2. Short Project Description:
Semi Colon is an online store specializing in selling digital gift cards for a wide range of international and local brands. Users can easily browse, purchase, and send gift cards either for themselves or as gifts to others, providing a fast and seamless experience similar to lilecard.

3. Project Goals:
Provide a simple, secure platform for purchasing digital gift cards.

Offer a fast, reliable, and user-friendly shopping experience.

Support multiple payment and delivery options.

Build customer trust in Semi Colon as a go-to brand for digital gifting.

4. Target Audience:
Individuals looking for quick and easy gift solutions.

People who prefer digital over physical gifts.

Corporate clients seeking bulk gift card solutions for employees or promotions.

5. Key Features:
Wide selection of gift cards (entertainment, gaming, retail, food, etc.).

Instant delivery via email or SMS.

Customizable messages with each gift card.

Secure online payments (credit cards, e-wallets, etc.).

User account management (order history, saved favorites, etc.).

Promotional offers and discount codes.

6. Design and Branding:
Clean, modern, and minimalistic design.

Colors that reflect trust, simplicity, and joy.

Mobile-first responsive design.

7. Competitors:
lilecard

G2A Gift Cards

Amazon Gift Cards

8. Success Metrics:
Number of users and repeat customers.

Conversion rate from browsing to purchase.

Customer satisfaction and feedback.

Growth in brand partnerships.

"
